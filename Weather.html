Ceylon Weather

Sri Lanka enjoys a tropical climate influenced by two major monsoons, the Southwest from May to September and the Northeast from December to February. This creates distinct wet and dry zones, while the central highlands remain cool and misty compared to hot, humid coastal lowlands. Because rainfall is seasonal and uneven, ancient Sri Lankan civilization developed a remarkable reservoir system known as the wewa chain. Built by kings like Parakramabahu, thousands of interconnected tanks and canals capture monsoon rain, store it, and distribute it during droughts, sustaining paddy farming, drinking water, biodiversity, and still forming the backbone of rural life
